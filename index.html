Why Arbiter

A wallet that trades.

Most wallets stop at holding. Most exchanges want custody. Arbiter does both ends properly — your keys never leave the device, and the order book is one tap from your balance.

🔑

You hold the keys

Your seed is generated on device and never leaves it. No account, no email, no custodian standing between you and your funds.

📈

Perps, natively

Long or short with leverage on Hyperliquid's order book. Market, limit, stop-loss, take-profit and stop-limit, all signed locally.

🛡️

Protect a position

Attach a take-profit and a stop-loss to any open position in two taps, or close it at market. Positions show at a glance what's guarding them.

💳

Buy with a card

Fund your wallet with USDC or ETH on Arbitrum directly from the app, through regulated on-ramp partners.

🔁

Swap across chains

Move between assets through several exchange providers at once, and take whichever quote is best at the moment you trade.

⚡

One balance, one app

Wallet balance and trading balance side by side. Deposit to Hyperliquid and withdraw back without leaving Arbiter.

Trading

Built for the position, not the pitch.

The trading side is a real client, not a widget. Live order book, candles across four timeframes, and every order type Hyperliquid supports.

  • ✓Up to 40× leverage depending on the market, set per position.
  • ✓Take-profit and stop-loss attached to the position, sized to close it in full.
  • ✓Signed on your device. Orders are EIP-712 signed locally and sent straight to Hyperliquid.
  • ✓Your history stays yours. Fills, deposits and withdrawals, read from the chain.
