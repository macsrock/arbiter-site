1. What Arbiter is

Arbiter is a non-custodial wallet for the Arbitrum network with trading on Hyperliquid built in. Keys are generated on your device and stored on your device. We never hold, control or have access to your funds, your seed phrase or your private keys.

We cannot recover your wallet. If you lose your seed phrase and lose access to your device, your funds are gone permanently. No one at Cake Labs can restore them, reverse a transaction, or freeze an address.

2. Fees

Arbiter charges a builder fee of 0.01% of order value on each trade you place through the app. The fee is collected by Hyperliquid at the protocol level and paid to Arbiter. It is charged in addition to Hyperliquid's own trading fees, which we do not receive.

Before your first trade, the app signs a one-time authorisation permitting this fee, as Hyperliquid requires. The current rate is always stated in the latest version of these terms.

Other charges you may encounter, none of which are ours:

  • Network fees. Arbitrum gas, paid to the network, for on-chain transactions such as deposits.
  • Exchange spreads and fees. Swaps route through third-party providers, whose quotes include their own margin. Arbiter may receive a share of that margin, which is reflected in the quote you are shown before you confirm.
  • On-ramp fees. Card and bank purchases are processed by third-party providers on their own terms and pricing.

We may change our fees. Changes take effect for trades placed after the updated rate appears in the app.

Markets can move faster than orders execute. Stop-losses are not guaranteed to fill at the trigger price, and in fast markets may fill materially worse. Network congestion, exchange downtime or connectivity loss may prevent an order being placed, modified or cancelled at all.
